我以**计算机/人工智能方向复试面试官**的视角，完全围绕你这份自述，整理出**最可能被问到的高频问题**，分中文+英文，按「背景→经历→项目→技术→研究规划」排序，都是复试现场大概率会追问的点：

# 一、本科专业与背景类
1. Why did you choose to pursue postgraduate studies in computer science/AI, even though your undergraduate major is microelectronics?
   你本科是微电子专业，为什么选择跨考计算机/人工智能方向读研？
2. How do your undergraduate courses (Biosensors, Integrated Circuit Design) relate to your future research interests?
   你本科的生物传感器、集成电路设计课程，和你未来的研究方向有什么关联？

# 二、工程实践经历类
3. Could you briefly talk about what you did in embedded firmware development?
   简单介绍下你做嵌入式固件开发的具体工作内容？
4. What’s the connection between PCB design, embedded firmware and software projects in your experience?
   你的PCB设计、嵌入式固件和软件项目之间有什么技术关联？
5. You have experience across hardware and software fields. Which part do you think lays the foundation for your current research interests?
   你横跨软硬件领域，你觉得哪部分经历为你现在的研究兴趣打下了基础？

# 三、独立项目深挖类（面试官最爱问）
6. Please introduce your automatic programming Agent in detail. What framework or model did you use?
   详细介绍你的自动编程Agent，用了什么框架或模型？
7. What’s the difference between the Seq2Seq-based QA system and the knowledge graph-based QA system?
   基于Seq2Seq的问答系统和基于知识图谱的问答系统，核心区别是什么？
8. Did you encounter any difficulties when developing these three projects? How did you solve them?
   开发这三个项目时遇到过什么困难，你是如何解决的？

# 四、技术栈与业余爱好类
9. You mentioned you are interested in OS concepts and task scheduling models. Could you talk about what you’ve learned?
   你提到对操作系统概念和任务调度模型感兴趣，能简单说说你了解的内容吗？
10. Why are you familiar with 3D modeling and slicing software? How do you combine it with AI?
   你为什么熟悉3D建模与切片软件？打算如何把它和AI结合？
11. How do you use PyTorch, Django and Selenium in your projects?
   你在项目中是如何使用PyTorch、Django和Selenium的？

# 五、专利与竞赛类
12. Could you briefly introduce the content of your authorized patents?
   简单介绍下你授权专利的核心内容？
13. What did you gain from the academic competitions?
   学术竞赛给你带来了哪些收获？

# 六、研究兴趣与读研规划类（必问）
14. Why do you want to focus on AI-driven 3D modeling and automatic skeletal rigging?
   为什么想研究AI驱动的3D建模和自动骨骼绑定方向？
15. What fundamental theories do you want to explore in this field?
   你想在这个领域探究哪些基础理论？
16. What’s your plan during postgraduate study to achieve research publications?
   读研期间你打算如何实现发表学术成果的目标？
17. What kind of research do you expect to do under the guidance of our supervisors?
   你希望在我校导师的指导下开展什么样的研究？

# 七、综合动机类
18. Why do you want to build a comprehensive knowledge system through postgraduate studies?
   为什么想通过读研构建完整的知识体系？
19. What are your advantages compared with other candidates who major in computer science?
   相比于科班计算机考生，你的优势是什么？
